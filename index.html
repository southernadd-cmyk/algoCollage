Composition & paper ＋
About this experiment ＋

How far can the golden ratio govern a collage while its fragments remain unexpected?

The composition engine comes from ALGO/ART ↗. Its territories select subjects and large image regions. Another output supplies placement.

Objects & faces uses local recognition and Sobel edge-guided contours. Faces take priority over full-person boxes, and oversized selections are rejected. Missed subjects become small details, not whole-image cuts; the Cut map lets you select a missed face. The separate Large image fragments mode is available when you want broad photographic blocks.

Your pool and outlines are saved in this browser. Save a project to keep the images, outlines and seeds together in a portable file.
